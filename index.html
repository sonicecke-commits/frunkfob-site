Questions, answered

When can I get one? We're testing interest first. If enough people join the waitlist, we'll make the first batch, and the waitlist hears before anyone else.

What will it cost? The plan is about $20. The final price depends on what it costs to make well.

Will it fit my adapter? Not yet, because nothing is cut yet. Tell us which adapter you carry when you sign up. We'll name exact fits only after we've tested them.

Is this official? No. Frunk & Fob isn't made by, or connected to, any carmaker or charging network.

Does it change how I charge? No. It's a case. It holds the adapter between uses and does nothing to the charging itself.

Do I need an adapter at all? It depends on your car's charging port and the chargers you use. Your car's manual or maker will say which adapter, if any, they recommend.

Where does it go? Wherever you keep it now: the frunk, the glovebox, a door pocket or under the floor of the trunk.
