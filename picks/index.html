At home

A Level 2 charger on the wall, and somewhere tidy to park the plug.

  • Our pick for most cars Mid-range pick

    Emporia Level 2 charger

    Start here. It comes in J1772 and Tesla-plug versions, so it suits either car, and home is where most EV owners do most of their charging (US Department of Energy).

    Suits a garage or driveway

    The maker says up to 48 A when hardwired on a 60 A circuit (less on a plug-in). Its app tracks the energy each charge uses. Choose the J1772 or Tesla-plug version for your car when you check the price.

  • Premium pick

    Autel MaxiCharger 50A Level 2 charger

    Suits a J1772 car and a garage or driveway with a hardwired install

    The maker says up to 50 A when hardwired, with a 25 ft cable and an outdoor-rated case. The listing says it's CSA certified. Trade-off: it's hardwired only, so you'll need an electrician to fit it.

    Lectron Nexus Level 2 charger (Tesla plug)

    Suits a Tesla-plug (NACS) car and a hardwired install

    The maker says up to 48 A when hardwired, with a 23 ft cable and a plug holder, and says it's UL 2594 listed. Trade-off: it's hardwired only, and it's a newer listing, so fewer owners have reviewed it so far.

On the road

Adapters for when the station's plug isn't yours, and air for when the tire's soft.
